# Conclusion Draft

#paper #conclusion #draft

This work validates a point-cloud-based tunnel deformation workflow using a controlled T0-T5 synthetic benchmark with known ground-truth deformation. The Step 6 workflow compares each target epoch against the baseline T0, computes section-level deformation metrics, and generates M3C2/time-series spatial evidence.

The benchmark passed all verification checks and recovered cumulative crown deformation with high accuracy. In the final T0-T5 comparison, the measured crown maximum was `44.05 mm` against an expected `45.00 mm`, giving an absolute error of `0.95 mm`. Across T1-T5, the workflow recovered the monotonic deformation trend and produced reproducible figures and benchmark outputs.

These results support the use of the workflow for controlled synthetic validation of tunnel time-series deformation monitoring. Future work should extend the validation to more realistic raycasting scenes, stronger registration stress, operational clutter, and real tunnel monitoring data.

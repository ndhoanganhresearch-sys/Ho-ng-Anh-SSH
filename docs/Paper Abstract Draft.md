## Draft Abstract

Time-series point-cloud monitoring provides a promising basis for quantifying tunnel deformation, but validation is challenging because real monitoring data rarely includes precise ground truth. This study develops and evaluates a tunnel deformation workflow using a controlled T0-T5 synthetic benchmark with known cumulative deformation. The workflow extracts a baseline centerline from the reference epoch, computes per-section deformation indicators, and evaluates cumulative crown deformation, convergence, and M3C2-based spatial distance response across multiple epochs. In the T0-T5 benchmark, the Step 6 deformation workflow recovered crown maximum deformation with exact recovery for T1-T2 and less than 1 mm absolute error for T3-T5. The final T0-T5 comparison measured 44.05 mm crown deformation against a 45.00 mm ground-truth value. These results demonstrate that the workflow can accurately track controlled multi-epoch tunnel deformation, while additional validation is needed for real tunnel scans with occlusion, registration drift, and operational clutter.
